About Us

LexRAH is a legal services firm headquartered in the National Capital Region (NCR), providing support in litigation, arbitration, regulatory documentation, and corporate legal matters. With a structured team of 15 qualified lawyers and a Pan-India litigation coordination network, LexRAH works closely with in-house legal teams, counsels, and institutions requiring focused and dependable legal assistance. Our approach prioritises legal clarity, procedural integrity, and confidentiality.
